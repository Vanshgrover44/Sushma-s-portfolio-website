01 — Payment

Advance Payment Model

PixelPressMedia works on an advance-payment model. Payment is collected before the agreed service or project work begins, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Services are initiated after the required advance payment has been received.

02 — Timeline

Project Delivery

The normal working timeline for services is approximately 2 weeks. Some projects may take longer due to their complexity, number of pages, revisions, technical work, or other requirements.

Where a client is slow to provide content, images, files, access, approvals, corrections, feedback, or responses, the delivery timeline may extend accordingly.

Two weeks is therefore a general working estimate and not a fixed guaranteed date unless expressly confirmed in writing.

03 — Cancellation

Cancellation Terms

Cancellation requests made within 3 days of payment may be eligible for a refund of up to 50% of the amount paid.

Cancellation requests made after 3 days are not eligible for a cancellation refund.

Where work has already been substantially performed, delivered, published, approved, or completed, the payment remains non-refundable.

04 — Client Cooperation

Information & Approvals

Clients are expected to provide the information and approvals needed to keep the project moving.

  • Delayed responses can extend the delivery timeline.
  • Missing content or access can pause work.
  • Scope changes can affect the quoted price and timeline.
  • Client-caused delays do not automatically create a refund right.
05 — Digital Services

Electronic Delivery

PixelPressMedia provides digital/electronic services. No physical goods are supplied unless specifically agreed in writing.
